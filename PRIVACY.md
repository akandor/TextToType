# TextToType Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

TextToType does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

- Clipboard: TextToType reads the text on your clipboard only at the moment you press your shortcut or choose "Type Clipboard". The text is typed into the app you have in front and is not saved, logged or transmitted. Depending on your settings, TextToType clears the clipboard after typing (for example when a password manager marked the text as a password).
- Accessibility permission: used solely to send keystrokes to the app you are typing into.
- Network: TextToType makes no network connections.
- Analytics and tracking: none.
- Settings (your shortcut, typing speed and clipboard clearing) are stored locally on your Mac.

Questions: https://github.com/akandor/TextToType/issues
